A2 BD-A2-103 (URGENT, LA-E3-02 / LA-ADR-05 rule 7, lane L): the exact rule.
CSP of the storage-check page (the 200 page that POST /lti/launch/{account} returns when the cookie is missing and a storage target exists):
  default-src 'none'; script-src 'nonce-<n>'; frame-ancestors https:; form-action 'self' <T>; base-uri 'none'
- <T> = the ORIGIN (scheme://host[:port]) of the tool URL that this storage check will 303 to: the account's launchUrl for a resource link launch, or its deepLinkingUrl for a Deep Linking launch. Both come from the resolved account (LA-ADR-06 D2, the account resolver; for cogniate both are the PUBLIC_APP_URL origin).
- It is computed per request from the prepared launch that the check row stores. One origin only, never a wildcard, never "https:".
- If the two tool URLs of the account have different origins, use the origin of the one that this check redirects to.
Why it is safe: CSP3 form-action also covers the redirect chain of a form submission, so 'self' alone blocks our own 303 to the tool. The form still posts only to our LTI origin, and the only extra origin is the account's own tool URL. That URL is operator-set, https, and never our LTI or content host (D2), and it is where the launch goes anyway. Everything else is unchanged: frame-ancestors https:, Referrer-Policy same-origin (the POST must carry our Origin), the nonce script, and no other form target.
No other LTI service page needs a change: the login page has form-action 'none' and navigates by script (not a form); the error pages have no form. The app's own pages (picker auto-submit) are not affected.
