## Goal

Each tool account has a claim policy (`allowlist` or `passthrough`, with `keep` groups) on top of the profile's privacy floor. The launch keeps only the claims that the policy and the floor allow. After the build, the operator sets the `cogniate` account to `allowlist` with every `keep` group off, so Moodle launches stop storing names, e-mail, `custom` and `lis` (D-9).

## Scope

In:
- The effect of D7: `passthrough` keeps every claim; `allowlist` keeps the ADR-011 §9 allowlist plus the `keep` groups (`names`, `email`, `custom`, `lis`).
- The floor: a profile with `privacyFloor: 'allowlist'` always applies the allowlist with every `keep` group off; a policy can remove more, never fewer.
- `null` policy = `passthrough` (old accounts). New accounts default to `allowlist` with every `keep` off (set at create by LA-E5-04).
- Outputs: `GET /api/idtoken` (also `raw=true`) shows only kept claims; `launch.custom` is `{}` when `custom` is removed; `launch_claims_minimized {platform_id, claims}` for every profile when the launch removes a claim.

Out: storing and validating the field (LA-E5-04); the NRPS fields (unchanged, contract §8 D13).

## Design notes

- The operator step after the build: `PATCH /operator/v1/accounts/cogniate {claimPolicy: {mode: "allowlist", keep: {names: false, email: false, custom: false, lis: false}}}`. It makes LA-IC-08 visible. QA makes its reviewed test-change list at this step (plan LD-9): stored Moodle claims, `GET /api/idtoken` (`raw=true`) for Moodle, tests that read Moodle `custom`, `lis`, name or e-mail (QA scan).
