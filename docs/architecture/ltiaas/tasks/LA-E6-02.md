## Goal

A library user and support can see why a connection fails without a log search. `GET /app-api/connections/:id/health` shows the last launch, the last launch refusal, the dispatch state, recent errors and the key state. The view also tells a Canvas admin when the inline key in the developer key JSON is out of date.

## Scope

- In: the `connection_event` table and writer (at most 100 rows per connection, 30 days, LA-H4); `artifact_key_kid`; the health route with the fields of LA-ADR-08 D5; the portal warnings ("Paste a new {LMS} key JSON" when `artifactKeyCurrent` is false; a warning when the oldest pending dispatch is older than 1 hour).
- Out: the operator command `app:connections health --stale-artifact-key` (optional, Could inside Could; build only if the Lead asks); alerts (operability §5).

## Design notes

- Ratified design points (BD-A2-100; LA-ADR-08 D5 "Build notes"): (a) `nextKid` is `null`; (b) `artifactKeyCurrent` is `null`, `true` or `false` as written; (c) the `check` event code format `ok` or `jwks:<detail> token:<detail>`, with `lastCheck` derived from it; (d) `lastError.code` is the parsed `CODE` or `null`, never free text; (e) at most 100 events, newest first; (f) no disconnect or suspend events in LA (deferred); (g) a collapsed health section with no `role="alert"`, and the M2 and T6 selectors unchanged.
- The view is read only and makes no outbound call: the public JWKS read (active and next kid) is cached 60 s.
- The writer trims a connection to its newest 100 rows in the same transaction as the insert; the 30-day rule runs with the app retention job.
