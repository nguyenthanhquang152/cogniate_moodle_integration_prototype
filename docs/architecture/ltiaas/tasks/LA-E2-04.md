## Goal

An operator grant for an admin-value channel binds every admin value, and the redemption refuses any value that differs. A Dynamic Registration that breaks I-SELF continues in the app as a staged registration: the pending connection switches once to `operatorGrant`, the operator confirms the staged values, and the redemption adopts and activates the staged platform. After this task `lti-generic` passes the whole kit.

## Scope

- In: `bound_values` written by `app:grant issue`; the flags `--issuer`, `--auth-url`, `--token-url`, `--jwks-url`, `--token-audience`, `--accept-staged`, `--confirm-foreign-hosts`; redemption compare and `422 GRANT_MISMATCH` with `details.fields`; the staged path of `PUT …/platform` (`{clientId, deploymentId, grantCode}`, adoption rule A3); the authority switch with `lms = 'canvas'` in one transaction; the `/tool/register` staged page; `app:grant list` prints bound values.
- Out: the LTI service side (LA-E2-02); A2's audit rows of these commands (LA-E5-11, LA-E5-12).

## Design notes
