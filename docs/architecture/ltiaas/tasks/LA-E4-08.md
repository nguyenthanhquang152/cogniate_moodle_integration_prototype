## Goal

On a channel with `content.ungraded` true, a placement launched without an AGS `lineitem` claim is ungraded: a final outcome makes a learning record but no dispatch, and the results row says "Not graded in {LMS}" without Resend. Channels without `content.ungraded` (Moodle) keep today's rule: a `failed` "No gradebook column" row.

## Scope

- In: the D2 rule (a new placement without the claim gets `graded = 0`; a later launch with the claim sets `1`; never back to `0`), only for channels with `content.ungraded`; `appliesTo` = `graded`; the results row text and no Resend for ungraded rows.
- Out: the picker rule (A2 LA-E3-08) and the manifest values and Canvas `link_selection` placement (A2 LA-E3-14). The three tasks are cut together.
