## Goal

The portal builds its channel registry from the manifests with `import.meta.glob`. It renders the Integrations cards, one route pair per channel and the four wizard steps ("show, collect, verify, enable") from the manifest. The Moodle, Canvas and Blackboard UI moves into `packages/channels/*/portal/` and imports only the portal SDK. Nothing visible changes.

## Design notes

- First move the helpers that the modules need into `apps/portal/src/sdk/`, then move the modules (LA-ADR-01 D5). A module that needs more than the SDK gets an SDK addition here, never a deep import.
- Static cards that are not channels (Kajabi, Figma, …) stay in the shell.
- Card logo and text (A1 BD-A1-111; QA-E2E LA-E7-07 round 1 P1): a card shows the manifest `card.title` and `card.description`; its logo is the named `card.icon` when the manifest sets one (BD-A1-49; Moodle: `graduation-cap`, an SVG), else `card.mark`. The Moodle card text is its manifest description, which is the frozen T6/M2 text "Export SCORM packages and sync course content with your Moodle LMS." (AC-04 "keep their title, text, mark and colour").
- Readiness (A1 BD-A1-51; Reviewer-A A-LA-E1-11-03): `ga` and `preview` channels render at once from manifest-derived fallback profiles; an answer of `GET /app-api/lms-providers` with `ready: false` shows `texts.notReady`, and its configured values (for example the Blackboard Client ID) are used; a failing call never hides a `ga` or `preview` card. One shell test: with the call failing, the Moodle card and its connect route still work; a later `ready: false` shows the not-ready text.
- Logo and visible changes (A1 BD-A1-49, BD-A1-50; Reviewer-A A-LA-E1-11-01, -02): the Moodle card and Connect page keep the graduation-cap icon through the new optional manifest field `card.icon: 'graduation-cap'` (closed list in the kernel schema; the Moodle manifest value by RQ-1 to K or one RQ-2 commit to P; one shell test: the Moodle card renders the icon, not the letter). This task ships LA-IC-18: the publish modal names the connection's LMS, the Connect-page logo uses the card colour, the logo has a tooltip. The "Open {LMS}" button stays in its old card (next to "Go to Courses").
- Wizard rule (A1 BD-A1-44; Reviewer-B B-LA-E1-11-01): the Moodle, Canvas and Blackboard modules keep their whole-wizard override (no visible change); the shell's four manifest steps are the wizard of every channel without a module `Wizard`. Every wizard selects its verify step from `connection.bindingAuthority` (before create: the chosen method's authority), never from `connection.method` or step JSON. `ChannelProvider` defaults `modules` to `channelModules`; a test that needs no module passes `new Map()`. Route tests render `App` with the real modules for the three baseline channels.
- Build rulings (LA-ADR-01 D5 P11 sketch and D9): BD-A1-24 the verify step reads the DTO field `bindingAuthority` (K adds it in LA-E1-07, RQ-1); BD-A1-25 the logo from `manifest.card`, the registered status text from `manifest.texts.registered` (K adds the field, RQ-1), and the optional slots `ConnectionError`, `ConnectionDetails`, `publicationPanels` and the `coursePanels` context (`version`, `onChanged`); BD-A1-26 a `simulation-only` channel shows a card and routes only when `GET /app-api/lms-providers` lists it.
