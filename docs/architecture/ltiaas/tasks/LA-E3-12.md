## Goal

Three small gaps of R1 close: `scoreGiven: null` means "no score"; `/api/memberships` checks the NRPS `service_versions`; a launch with an untrusted extra audience fails check 5.

## Scope

In:
- AGS-15: `scoreGiven: null` in a score POST is treated as absent (today `400`).
- NR-01: `/api/memberships` gives `403 SERVICE_NOT_AVAILABLE` when the claim has `service_versions` without `2.0`; a claim without `service_versions` passes (Moodle).
- S-08: platform field `trustedAudiences` (list, default `[]`) on create, `PATCH` and in answers; check 5 refuses an `aud` value that is not the client id and not in the list.

Out: NRPS `differences` and the member `message` section; `lti_msg`; custom content item types (LA-ADR-05 D10 "Not in LA").

## Design notes

- LA-IC-13 ships here. QA makes its reviewed test-change list at this task (plan LD-9): tests that expect `400` for `scoreGiven: null` (QA scan).
- Default `[]` refuses extra audiences. No known baseline platform sends them; Moodle sends one value.
