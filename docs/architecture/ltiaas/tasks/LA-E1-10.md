## Goal

Finish the LTI service part of E1 in the files that lane S owned in B1. `GET /api/providers` adds `channel` to each profile and takes the frozen `lms` value from `wireLms`. The admin-value create plan reads the `endpoints` trait at its call sites. Every LTI service error and registration page fills `{LMS}` from the manifest; Moodle texts stay byte for byte.
