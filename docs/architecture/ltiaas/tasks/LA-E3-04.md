## Goal

The LTI service accepts an empty Deep Linking response ("Cancel"), honours the platform's `accept_lineitem` setting, and shows `acceptLineItem` and `acceptMediaTypes` in the shaped token. The app part (Cancel button, multi-select, the not-graded page) is LA-E3-05.

## Scope

In:
- D3a service: `contentItems: []` is valid and signs a response with `content_items: []`; with `accept_multiple: false`, 0 or 1 item is valid; the maximum of 20 items stays. The rules hold for `POST /api/deeplinking`, `/api/deeplinking/message` and `/api/deeplinking/form`.
- D3c service: when the launch's `deep_linking_settings.accept_lineitem` is `false`, an item with `lineItem` gives `400 INVALID_CONTENT_ITEM` ("the platform does not accept line items").
- D3d: `launch.deepLinkingSettings` adds `acceptLineItem` and `acceptMediaTypes` when the platform sends `accept_lineitem` and `accept_media_types`.

Out: per-type item fields D3e (LA-E3-13, Should); the app picker (LA-E3-05).

## Design notes

- With `accept_multiple: false`, two or more items give `400 INVALID_CONTENT_ITEM` (the existing code).
- LA-IC-12 (service part) ships here. QA makes its reviewed test-change list at this task (plan LD-9): a test that expects `400` for an empty list, and whole-object checks of `deepLinkingSettings` (QA scan).
