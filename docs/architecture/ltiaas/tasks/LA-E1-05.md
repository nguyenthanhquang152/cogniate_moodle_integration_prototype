## Goal

Move the LTI profiles of Moodle, Canvas and Blackboard into `packages/channels/*/lti-profiles.ts`. The LTI service builds its profile registry from the channel folders, checks each profile against one schema with a closed trait set, and runs generic check 14 from the manifests' guarded families. `PUBLIC_MOODLE_URL` leaves the services. Wire answers and launch behaviour do not change.

## Scope

- In: `ProfileSource` (P10); `channel` set by the loader; traits `endpoints` (replaces `isStandardProfile` inside `profiles/index.ts`, so the call sites in `platforms.ts` and `service-context.ts` keep their results), `privacyFloor`, `wireLms`, `listOrder`; load checks 2, 5 (LTI side) and 7; generic check 14 with both guard levels and the `MANUAL_SELF_LEGACY_CHANNELS` rule; `lmsNameOf` and `lmsNameOfFamily` as registry lookups; LA-IC-02.
- Out: the `channel` field on `GET /api/providers` and the trait swap at the `platforms.ts` call sites (LA-E1-10, B2, when L owns that file); LTI page texts (LA-E1-10); E2 traits (LA-E2-01).

## Design notes

- The `ProviderProfile` schema and its closed trait set live in `services/lti-service/src/profiles/` (lane L), not in the kernel package: load check 7 runs in the LTI profile registry.
- Until LA-E1-10, `GET /api/providers` keeps today's fields; the LTI service fills `lms` from `wireLms` inside the profile object, so `platforms.ts:309` gives the same value.
