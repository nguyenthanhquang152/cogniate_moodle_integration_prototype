## Goal

The LTI service runs its scheduled work on the one job runner of LA-ADR-03 D7 (instance `lti-1`), not on its own timer. This task wires the LTI runner (start, stop, the instance heartbeat) and adds the LTI tables `job_lease` and `job_instance`. It runs three Must jobs: `retention` (today's cleanup, moved), `audit-retention` (deletes audit events older than `AUDIT_RETENTION_DAYS`) and `signing-key-cleanup` (the job module of LA-E5-10, S2; this task registers it). Each run has one lease, one trace id and one `job_run` line.

## Scope

- In: the runner wiring (instance `lti-1`, boot id, heartbeat row, delete on stop; LA-ADR-03 D7, Q-LAD-23); `retention` (at start, then every 10 minutes; the `retention_cleanup` lines stay as today; LA-E3-02 (L) adds the call of its `storage_check` cleanup function by RQ-1 when it lands); `audit-retention` (daily; `AUDIT_RETENTION_DAYS`, default 400); the registration of `signing-key-cleanup` (module and its rules: LA-E5-10).
- Out: `key-rewrap` (LA-E5-09); the Should jobs and `/operator/v1/jobs` (LA-E5-20); the app's retention (the app runner is R's).

## Design notes

- `audit-retention` is the only product code that may delete from `audit_event` (fitness rule F5, from LA-E5-02; LA-ADR-06 D4 rule 2). The append-only trigger refuses `UPDATE`, not `DELETE`.
- The instance rule of LA-ADR-03 D7 (boot id, delete on stop, same-slot restart keeps today's timing) holds for the LTI service runner too ([operability §2](../operability.md#2-configuration)).
