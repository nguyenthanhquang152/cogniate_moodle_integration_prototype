## Design notes

- **Merge mode.** LA-H10 "frozen candidate": merge mode `wave-end`. The B/C run's product commits that passed their own QA are merged at the end of each LA wave. A change in a file that E1 moved (for example `services/cogniate-app/src/providers/canvas.ts`) goes into the new channel folder file (LA-ADR-04 D9). LA-H10 "wait for full acceptance": merge mode `none`; G-BASE is the final B/C SHA.
- **Golden after a merge.** A merged B/C change can change a golden field on purpose. Then QA re-captures the golden baseline at the merged SHA (LA-E0-03) and lists each changed field with its B/C commit. The next LA step compares against the new capture.
- Lane K does the merge because K owns most moved files. Other lanes rebase their open branches on the merged SHA.
