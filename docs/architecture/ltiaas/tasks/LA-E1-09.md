## Goal

Every app, player and tracking text that names an LMS uses `{LMS}` with the manifest text name. Closed name unions (`'Moodle' | 'Canvas' | 'Blackboard'`) become `string`. Moodle, Canvas and Blackboard texts stay byte for byte. The app serves the portal routes for every registry channel id.

## Scope

- In: D12 texts; the player name from the play view; the app server allowlist `/integrations/:channelId/connect` and `/integrations/:channelId/:connectionId` from the registry (D9 routes).
- Out: LTI service page texts (LA-E1-10); portal texts (LA-E1-11); DR page texts for new channels (LA-E2-02).
