## Goal

On a channel with `content.ungraded` true, a teacher can add a Cogniate course as an ungraded placement (Canvas module item through `link_selection`). The picker sends the item without `lineItem`; the placement is not graded, so a final outcome makes a learning record but no dispatch, and the results row says "Not graded in {LMS}". Moodle keeps today's rule (a `failed` "No gradebook column" row).

## Scope

In: the picker rule. On a channel with `content.ungraded` true, the picker sends `ltiResourceLink` items without `lineItem` when the launch has `acceptLineItem === false`, or when the signed `launch.target` has the query parameter `cogniate_placement=ungraded`. On other channels the rule of LA-E3-05 (`PLACEMENT_NOT_GRADED`) stays.

Out: LA-E3-14 (K) and LA-E4-08 (R); Blackboard (false until a BLACKBOARD-LIVE check).
