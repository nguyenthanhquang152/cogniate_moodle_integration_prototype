## Goal

An LMS administrator connects an LMS to Cogniate with one guide per channel. The two new guides cover `lti-generic` (Dynamic Registration and manual connect with an approval code) and D2L Brightspace (Register Tool, deployment, links). The Moodle, Canvas and Blackboard guides get only the LA changes that QA verified.

## Scope

- In: the tool configuration rows with their keys; the approval-code step; the staged registration text ("Ask your Cogniate operator for the approval code."); the D2L defaults and the UNVERIFIED facts; the Moodle `standard` limit for non-Moodle LMSs.
- Out: operator steps (LA-E8-04).
