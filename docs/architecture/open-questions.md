# Open questions for humans

These decisions belong to people, not to the team of agents. Each has our recommendation. The prototype uses the recommendation until a human decides. The Lead moves the open items to `docs/human_review/decisions-needed.md`.

"Blocks" = what waits for the answer.

| # | Area | Question | Recommendation | Who decides | Blocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Legal | (a) Is our process acceptable (ADR-001 clean-room table: only the Architect read the public LTIAAS reference; Author and QA work from our contract)? (b) May QA use the MIT `ltiaas` Python SDK as a **test-only** black-box client (T3c, AC-T3-27), without copying its code? (c) How may we describe the result? | (a) Yes, keep the provenance in the contract. (b) Yes: MIT allows use; it is not shipped. (c) Use only the claim in ADR-003 ("implements the listed endpoints with the paths, header schemes and field names of the public LTIAAS API reference, except the listed differences, and passes a test with an independent client"). Do not use "LTIAAS" in product names. Check the LTI® mark and plan 1EdTech membership and certification. | Legal | External claims, T3c start |
| Q2 | Product | Launch in a **new window** or **embedded** in Moodle? | New window (ADR-005). An embedded launch shows an "Open in a new window" page. | Product | Admin guide text |
